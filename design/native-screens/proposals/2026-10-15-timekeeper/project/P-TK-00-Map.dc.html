Timekeeper mode: map

Timekeeper mode puts a third, neutral, signed-in person in charge of a ranked match: they start it, run the clock and record the result, while every athlete safeguard stays (opponent weight check, confirm or dispute, the 24 h auto-confirm, video from any phone). It sits behind the flag timekeeper_role, snapshotted per match, and the flag-OFF flow does not change.

What changed (timekeeper mode only)
Flag ON only: every NEW match whose two athletes' clients advertise timekeeper support requires a timekeeper (matches.requires_timekeeper, snapshot at creation). Otherwise today's two-athlete flow, untouched.
After Accept both athletes land on a new step crew: pick a timekeeper from friends of either athlete and live athletes nearby (Q12: the other athlete approves), and the asked person accepts on their phone. The code/QR join stays as the in-person fallback under the same rules.
A 10-minute deadline from accept; nobody accepted means the server cancels the match, no rating.
Athlete face-off: weigh-in unchanged; ready merges the opponent weight check (Weight looks right, I'm ready), stored server side.
The timekeeper confirms each athlete's weight and alone can Start, pause, resume, end the clock (new end_match_clock; the server also ends it at match duration) and record the result.
Athletes keep their camera, see a read-only clock, then confirm or dispute; the recorder is no longer auto-confirmed.
D12: the timekeeper MUST record the match (consent at Accept, record row locked on, Start waits for their camera, upload required; missing footage = admin flag, Q14 decided). Athletes' recording stays optional.
An explicit Leave pauses the clock; an OFFLINE timekeeper (60 s heartbeat gap) starts a 10-minute wait without pausing; cancel only if nobody takes over. After the clock ends there is no Leave; a result stall goes to admin review.
Arena copy changes in two lines when the flag is known ON (TK-AR1, TK-AR2).

Risks
Public broadcast channel (session-match:<id>, jr_be-czt): server gates never trust broadcasts; ready, end and drop-out move to the server in timekeeper mode.
Stale pending matches: the 10-minute cancel covers timekeeper mode only; flag-OFF stale pending stays jr_be-xpf.
pgTAP 061, 108 and 111 pin record-only behaviour: every change keys on requires_timekeeper so they pass unchanged.
The wizard assumes two participants: the timekeeper must never become a match_participants row (loser derivation, finalize count).
Older app builds: the capability rule keeps a mixed pair on the flag-OFF flow.
A friendly timekeeper seated by one athlete (Q12, plus admin signals for repeat pairs, drop-outs after the end and disputes per timekeeper).
Lock order: new writers lock matches first; the rating_v9 advisory lock is never taken after a match row lock.
Open questions for the owner, with recommended answers
Q1
Timekeeper flag blocks start until re-weigh and re-confirm?
Recommended: Yes, same as the athlete flag. PENDING OWNER.
Q2
Merge the opponent check into ready?
Recommended: Yes in timekeeper mode; flag OFF (C1) not before the alpha.
Q3
Explicit Leave during the clock: pause, wait 10 min for a replacement, then cancel, no rating?
Recommended: Yes. Rejected: hand the clock back to the athletes.
Q3b
Offline timekeeper: a heartbeat gap starts the 10-min wait without unseating or pausing; a drop-out only when it expires?
Recommended: Yes.
Q3c
Stalls: no start within 10 min of both ready, or no result within 10 min of the end, counts as offline; after the end, admin review, no Leave?
Recommended: Yes.
Q4
Code/QR as the in-person fallback?
Recommended: Keep on the backend; add Use a code instead (TK-P24) only after the picker ships. Not drawn.
Q5
Who can pick?
Recommended: Either athlete asks, either can Ask someone else; see Q12.
Q6
Video of a cancelled match?
Recommended: Keep it attached, visible to the athletes and the timekeeper who filmed it, no highlight.
Q7
Request lapse?
Recommended: 2 minutes inside the 10-minute deadline.
Q8
Phase 2 deadline?
Recommended: A claimed off-platform invite extends the deadline once by 10 minutes.
Q9
Timekeeper rating or credit?
Recommended: None in phase 1; a matches-timekept count later.
Q10
Alpha?
Recommended: Ship dark (go/no-go below).
Q11
Phase 2 invite side effects?
Recommended: Create the friendship; do not count against the challenge invite cap.
Q12
Should the other athlete approve a picked timekeeper (collusion)?
Recommended: Yes: one tap (TK-P25 to TK-P27, picker waits with TK-P28) inside the same 10 minutes, before the request is sent; also for replacements and code joins; admin signals.
Q13
Arena copy says a timekeeper is needed even if the other athlete's app is too old to support it; keep the copy?
Recommended: Yes: nothing breaks, the match falls back to the two-athlete flow.
Q14
Missing timekeeper footage (D12): the upload never arrives or fails?
DECIDED (owner 2026-10-15): the timekeeper's video is required; if it is missing, the result and rating stand and the match is flagged for admin review (signal only) at the 24 h settle window; one push nudge 2 h after the end.
