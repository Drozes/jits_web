Hold to end on the timekeeper's phone calls the new server action end_match_clock (BE-5), which stamps ended_at and the final clock and leaves the status in_progress; the legacy end_match refuses timekeeper-mode matches (use_end_match_clock). The athletes' phones freeze on that DB change (not a broadcast) and show the shipped ended plate with the timekeeper's name, then the result waiting view (board B8, frame 2).

Server auto-end (review M2): if the timekeeper has not ended the clock at full time (start plus duration plus paused time), the per-minute sweep stamps ended_at; the athletes' phones already show TIME. The 10-minute result window starts at ended_at.
