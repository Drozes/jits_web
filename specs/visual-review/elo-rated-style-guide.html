08
ANTI-PATTERNS
What not to do. These rules keep the system tight and prevent visual entropy. Every violation dilutes the brand and reduces data clarity.
  • × Drop shadows, glows, elevation effects. Hierarchy comes from value shifts between surface layers, not simulated lighting.
  • × Decorative gradients or motion. Gradients are reserved for brand hero treatments. Motion serves data communication only.
  • × Rounded pill badges. Use sharp 2-3px radius. Pill shapes (border-radius: 9999px) are never used on data surfaces or badges.
  • × Rating numbers in anything other than JetBrains Mono. All numeric data, all the time, no exceptions.
  • × Signal Red as decoration. Red is reserved for CTAs and state-negative indicators. Never for borders, backgrounds, or ambient color.
  • × Gain Green as decoration. Green is exclusively for rating increases and win states. Never for "success" toasts or generic positive feedback.
  • × Pure white (#FFFFFF) backgrounds. Even in Paddock (light theme), the lightest surface is #F2F4F7. Pure white breaks the tonal system.
  • × More than one primary CTA button per surface. One red button per card, per modal, per visible viewport. If two actions compete, one becomes secondary.
